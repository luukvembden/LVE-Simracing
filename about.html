About LVE

Made by a Racer.
For Racers.

It started with a teenager from the Netherlands who simply loved racing.

Motorsport had always been fascinating, but when he discovered sim racing, it opened up a whole new world. The idea of racing from home, learning tracks, improving lap times and getting closer to the real thing was incredible.

There was just one problem: sim racing can get expensive. Good hardware, dashboards and other equipment quickly add up, making it harder to get into the hobby without spending a lot of money.

So instead of accepting that, he decided to build his own.

What started as a personal project grew into LVE. The first step was creating free SimHub dashboards, giving other sim racers access to detailed, useful racing displays without having to spend anything.

But the idea didn't stop there.

LVE evolved from digital dashboards into real physical hardware. The goal is simple: create high-quality sim racing products that look and feel professional, while keeping them accessible to more people.

From a teenager in the Netherlands making his first dashboard to developing real hardware, LVE is built around one idea:

Making sim racing for everyone.

Sim racing hardware and digital solutions designed with a focus on quality, usability and accessible performance.
