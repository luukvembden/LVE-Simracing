THE BRAND

About LVE

LVE SimRacing is built around one simple idea: good sim racing hardware should be accessible without feeling cheap.

The Idea

Made by a Racer.
For Racers.

LVE started from a personal interest in sim racing and the desire to create hardware and digital tools that solve real problems. The brand combines automotive-inspired design with practical features.

The goal isn't to build the most expensive setup on the market. It's to create products that make sense, look right and improve the experience.

LVE Principles

What We Care About

◈

Design

◇
